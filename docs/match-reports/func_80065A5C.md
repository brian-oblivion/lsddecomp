### Proposed learning

A single `__asm__("")` at the top of a function is not guaranteed to fully
normalize prologue store order — it closed the *parameter-copy-deferral*
half of this residue (matching `func_80065D64`'s single-barrier fix) but
left a three-way store permutation among the *other* callee-saved
registers untouched. Whether one barrier suffices seems to depend on how
many independent store operations the scheduler is juggling in the
prologue, not just on whether a barrier exists at all — worth escalating
as a possible toolchain/scheduler question rather than continuing to grind
source-level permutations once a barrier alone doesn't close it fully.

### Proposed learning

**Barrier PLACEMENT, not just barrier presence, matters and is not
monotonic.** This function now has three data points for `__asm__("")`
positioning: no barrier (1/33, missing frame entirely), barrier as the
first statement (30/33, the best reached), and barrier one statement
later (5/33, with address drift). The middle position is not "between
the two extremes" in any predictable sense -- moving a no-op barrier by
one statement is not guaranteed to move the score monotonically or even
stay in the same failure mode (a missing-frame failure vs. an
address-drift failure are qualitatively different). Treat each barrier
position as an independent experiment; do not extrapolate from "the
first position I found helps" to "moving it slightly will help more or
less predictably."
