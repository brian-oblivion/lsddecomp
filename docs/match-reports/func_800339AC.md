## One slip: wrong mask constant

First pass wrote `&= ~3` (clearing bits 0 and 1). Retail's `addiu
$v1,$zero,-0x3` is `-3 = 0xFFFFFFFD = ~2`, clearing only bit 1 -- a plain
misread of `-3` as "clear the low two bits" rather than "the two's
complement of 3 IS `~2`". Diffed one word short until corrected to `&= ~2`.

### Proposed learning

A small negative constant loaded via `addiu $v1,$zero,-N` immediately before
an `and` is `~(N-1)`, not `~N` and not "clear the low `log2(N)` bits" --
convert `-N` to its two's complement bit pattern explicitly
(`-3 = 0xFFFFFFFD = ~2`) before writing the mask, rather than pattern-
matching the decimal value against a guessed bit count.
